01 // WHO WE ARE

About Panther Group

Panther Group is a MilSim gaming community formed in early 2021. We are a semi-serious group who primarily run PvP and PvE operations in ARMA Reforger and ARMA 3, but we also play a number of other games.

We are an Australian community, although members from all across the globe are welcome.

OUR FOCUS

Fostering and creating a strong MilSim community across a multitude of games.

02 // ORDER OF BATTLE

Detachments

The units operating under the Panther Group name. Lore below is in-game fiction for our campaigns.

OPFOR

Assault Recon Group «Panther»

  • Semi-autonomous detachment of Wagner Group
  • Shturmovaya Razvedyvatel'naya Gruppa «Pantera»
  • Штурмовая Разведывательная Группа «Пантера»

The Wagner Group (Russian: Группа Вагнера), officially known as PMC Wagner (ЧВК «Вагнер», ChVK “Vagner”), is a Russian state-funded private military company (PMC) that was controlled until 2023 by Yevgeny Prigozhin, a former close ally of Russia's president Vladimir Putin, and since then by Pavel Prigozhin. The Wagner Group has used infrastructure of the Russian Armed Forces.

From the onset of the SMO, Assault Reconnaissance Group “Panther” has been involved in some of the more infamous expeditions during their tenure in Ukraine. Conducting raids and specialising in sabotage, the unit acts as an independent force that, while privately funded under Wagner Group, works closely with MoD forces to achieve genuine strategic victories in the Special Military Operation.

The unit remains at large, as only four of its operators have ever been known to have been captured (as identified via their special patches). However, the unit's size remains largely a mystery due to their tight online presence. Plenty of videos surfaced after Bakhmut of effective FPV drone strikes and trench raids in the Chasov Yar direction in 2023.

The unit did not participate in the Wagner rebellion.

As of 2025, the unit has confirmed via its Telegram channel, through their commander Maxim “Akula” Bulganov, that they are operating in the Southern Pokrovsk direction, assisting in the disruption of Ukrainian logistics through special sabotage actions and FPV drone work.

BLUFOR

Separate Special Unit “Panther”

  • Detachment of the 12th Special Forces Brigade “Azov”
  • Okremyy Spetspidrozdil «Pantera»
  • Окремий Спецпідрозділ «Пантера»

The 12th Special Forces Brigade “Azov” is a professional military formation within the National Guard of Ukraine, founded on May 5, 2014. Azov has been protecting the territorial integrity of the state, its laws and constitutional order, and actively participates in repelling Russian aggression on the territory of Ukraine.

During the siege of Mariupol in the opening phase of the invasion of Ukraine, additional units were raised as part of Azov as a means to bolster defences and add operational capacity.

After the battle of Mariupol, some of the forces who joined the tactical retreat from Mariupol were reorganised and repurposed elsewhere on the front. One such group was the Separate Special Unit “Panther”, which consisted initially of volunteers and ideologically motivated veterans. In December 2022, during the fiercest and most dangerous fighting in Bakhmut, the Pantera group was deployed to reinforce the central defence effort.

While sustaining minimal casualties and inflicting heavy damage on the invader, the battle was eventually lost in May 2023. The Pantera group remained on the outskirts of Bakhmut and helped in the strenuous defence of Chasiv Yar until their rotation for refitting, which allowed new recruits to be trained in time for the Ukrainian general offensive in the Zaporizhzhia direction.

The group has been deployed to the Kursk offensive, and has played a key role in defending the Vovchansk direction.

Currently, Panther has been sent into the fire that is the Pokrovsk direction.
